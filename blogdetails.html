How To Know When Your Tyres Need Replacing.

Understanding the warning signs of tyre wear can help you make safer decisions, improve driving performance, and avoid unexpected problems on the road.

WRITTEN BY GripCore Experts
PUBLISHED July 18, 2026
READING TIME 6 Min Read

Your tyres are one of the most important safety components of your vehicle. They are the only point of contact between your vehicle and the road, which means their condition directly affects grip, braking, handling, and overall driving confidence.

Over time, tyres naturally wear down due to mileage, road conditions, weather, driving habits, and maintenance. Knowing when replacement is necessary can help prevent unexpected failures and keep your vehicle performing safely.

01

Check Your Tyre Tread Depth

Tread depth plays an important role in maintaining traction, especially when driving on wet roads. As tyres wear, the grooves become shallower and their ability to channel water away from the contact surface can decrease.

If the tread has become significantly worn or the tyre is approaching the legal minimum specified in your region, it is important to have it professionally inspected and consider replacement.

GRIPCORE SAFETY TIP

Do not rely only on tread depth. Cracks, bulges, cuts, and other visible damage can also indicate that a tyre needs professional attention.

02

Look For Cracks, Cuts, And Bulges

Physical damage is another important warning sign. Cracks may develop on the sidewalls due to age, weather exposure, or environmental conditions.

Bulges and bubbles can indicate internal structural damage. These issues should never be ignored because they may affect the structural integrity of the tyre.

03

Pay Attention To Uneven Tyre Wear

Uneven wear across the tyre surface can be a sign of alignment, balancing, suspension, or inflation issues.

If one side of the tyre is wearing faster than another, it is important to identify the underlying cause. Replacing the tyre without addressing the problem may result in the new tyre wearing unevenly again.

QUICK CHECK

Signs Your Tyres May Need Attention

  • Excessive or uneven tread wear
  • Visible cracks or cuts
  • Bulges or bubbles on the sidewall
  • Repeated loss of tyre pressure
  • Unusual vibration or handling changes
04

Notice Changes In Driving Performance

Changes in the way your vehicle feels can sometimes provide an early indication of tyre-related issues. Steering vibrations, unusual road noise, or changes in handling should be investigated.

These symptoms can have several possible causes, including tyre condition, wheel balancing, alignment, suspension, or other vehicle components. A professional inspection can help identify the actual cause.

05

Consider Tyre Age And Usage

Tyres can age even when they have not been driven extensively. Long periods of storage, exposure to sunlight, temperature changes, and environmental conditions can affect rubber compounds over time.

Check the tyre manufacturer's recommendations and have older tyres inspected regularly by a qualified professional.

GRIPCORE FINAL TAKEAWAY

Your Tyres Deserve Regular Attention.

Regular inspections, correct inflation, professional wheel alignment, and timely replacement can help you maintain a safer and more confident driving experience.
