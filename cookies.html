This Cookies Policy explains how GripCore uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, understand website usage, and provide reliable website services.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit and make your browsing experience smoother and more convenient.

How We Use Cookies

GripCore may use cookies to support essential website functions, remember preferences, understand website performance, and improve the services and content available to visitors.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies help important website features operate correctly. These cookies may be required for navigation, security, session functionality, and other basic website operations.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies may help us understand how visitors interact with the website, including which pages are viewed and how users navigate through our services. This information helps us improve website performance.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may remember settings such as language, display preferences, or other choices you make while using the GripCore website.

Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on third-party services that use cookies or similar technologies. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features or functionality.

Changes to This Policy

GripCore may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, technology, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page.
