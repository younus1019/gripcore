FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Find answers to common questions about our services, pricing, appointments, and vehicle care.

What services does GripCore offer?

GripCore provides professional tyre services, precision wheel alignment, wheel balancing, vehicle inspection, and expert automotive care.

How do I know which service my vehicle needs?

Our expert technicians can inspect your vehicle and recommend the right service based on tyre condition, wheel alignment, balancing, and overall vehicle performance.

Do I need to book an appointment?

We recommend booking an appointment in advance to help ensure a smooth and convenient service experience. You can book your visit through our online appointment form.

How often should I check my wheel alignment?

Wheel alignment should be checked regularly and whenever you notice uneven tyre wear, steering problems, or your vehicle pulling to one side.

How often should my wheels be balanced?

Wheel balancing is recommended when you experience steering or vehicle vibration, after installing new tyres, or whenever a wheel or tyre is removed and refitted.

Can I get a vehicle inspection before choosing a service?

Yes. Our vehicle inspection service helps identify potential tyre, wheel, and vehicle-related concerns so our technicians can recommend the appropriate care for your vehicle.
